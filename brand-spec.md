# Lia — brand and asset notes

## Identity

- **Logo:** `public/logo_lia.png` (original asset; do not redraw or recolor).
- **Primary teal:** `#0d9488`.
- **Deep teal:** `#134e4a`.
- **Accent amber:** `#f59e0b`; light accent `#fbbf24`.
- **Typography:** Inter, loaded from Google Fonts by `src/layouts/Layout.astro`.
